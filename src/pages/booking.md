<h2>Have this ready</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Your Mac's model.</strong> Apple menu &gt; About This Mac. If it won't turn on, the model number is printed on the underside of a MacBook or under the stand of an iMac.</li>
  <li><strong>What it is doing.</strong> When it started, and whether it followed a drop, a spill or an update.</li>
  <li><strong>Your data.</strong> Tell us if there are files you need kept.</li>
</ul>
